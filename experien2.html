HEALTH / CLARITY

01 / OVERVIEW

A more human
comparison.

Healthcare costs are hard to understand in isolation. This project explores a single place to compare services, pricing, providers, and supportive AI tools.

The problem

MedicoCompare brings consultation fees, diagnostic test prices, hospital services, and treatment costs into one experience. Its aim is to improve price transparency so people can more easily compare affordable healthcare options.
